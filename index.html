About Us

A contemporary Georgian art platform connecting artists and art lovers through meaningful visual experiences.

Ninart Vision is an independent art platform bringing together original works by Georgian artists. We created this space so both emerging and established Georgian painters can be represented in a unified creative environment — and their work can reach a wider audience. The platform also simplifies discovery and purchase of original works for collectors and art lovers in one place.
